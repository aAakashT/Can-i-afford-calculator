# Privacy launch checklist

- Keep client-side calculation as the default.
- Never log raw salary, debt, expenses, or savings.
- Do not put financial inputs into share URLs, analytics events, or error messages.
- Keep optional ads and analytics off until consent and policy requirements are reviewed.
- Replace the legal page templates with reviewed terms for the operating company, jurisdiction, retention, rights, and contact details.
- If a catalog or saved-history backend is introduced, document retention, deletion, access control, and breach response before launch.
